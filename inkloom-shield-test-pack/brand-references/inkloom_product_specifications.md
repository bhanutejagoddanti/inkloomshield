# Inkloom Product Specifications (Synthetic Test Reference)

This fictional specification is the approved source of truth for sample tests.

## Supported capabilities
- Users submit marketing copy for AI-assisted review.
- Review may flag unsupported factual claims, tone mismatches, and possible policy issues.
- The platform can suggest edits for human review.
- Brand Trust Score is a workflow indicator, not certification.

## Explicit limitations
- No guarantee of 100% accuracy.
- Does not replace all human reviewers.
- Does not guarantee legal or regulatory compliance.
- No evidence that Inkloom has been used by Fortune 500 companies since 2018.
- No approved source supports “better than all competitors.”
- Prices, discounts, customers, certifications, and event dates require a separate approved brief.

## Approved wording
“Inkloom helps marketing teams review content against brand guidance and approved product information. It flags potential issues and suggests changes for a human reviewer.”

## Disallowed wording
“Inkloom guarantees 100% accuracy for all content and replaces human reviewers completely.”
