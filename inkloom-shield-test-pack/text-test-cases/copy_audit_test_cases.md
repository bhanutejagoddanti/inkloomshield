# Inkloom Shield — Test Cases

Use the brand guidelines and product specifications in `brand-references/` as the source of truth. Upload each image from `marketing-creatives/`. OCR is not included in the current build, so paste the matching copy into the Creative Audit text field.

## TC-01 — Valid and compliant
**Expected result:** Pass or low-risk; clear professional tone.

> Inkloom helps marketing teams review content against brand guidance and approved product information. It flags potential issues and suggests changes for a human reviewer.

## TC-02 — Unsupported accuracy guarantee
**Expected result:** Flag 100% accuracy and replacement of human reviewers.

> Inkloom guarantees 100% accuracy for all content and can replace human reviewers completely.

## TC-03 — Unsupported customer/history claims
**Expected result:** Flag Fortune 500 and since-2018 claims as unsupported.

> Trusted by Fortune 500 companies since 2018, Inkloom has transformed content governance for every enterprise.

## TC-04 — Unsupported competitor superlative
**Expected result:** Flag absolute comparison and unsupported promise.

> Inkloom is better than all competitors and will always deliver the best results.

## TC-05 — Tone mismatch
**Expected result:** Flag slang-heavy exaggerated tone.

> Yo!!! This tool is insanely epic and will totally crush every other content platform 🚀🔥

## TC-06 — Unapproved discount
**Expected result:** Request approved offer brief; flag price/discount as unverified.

> Get 50% off today only! Our premium plan is now just $24. Guaranteed savings—buy now!

## TC-07 — Potential privacy concern
**Expected result:** Flag broad data/privacy disclaimer and suggest approved wording.

> We store no user data and are not responsible for any issues that may occur while using the platform.

## TC-08 — Event details require validation
**Expected result:** Check date/time against approved event brief.

> Join our webinar on March 15, 2025 at 11:00 AM IST: The Future of AI in Content Governance.

## TC-09 — Safe capability description
**Expected result:** Likely acceptable if aligned with specification.

> Create, review, and improve marketing content with AI-assisted checks for brand alignment and factual consistency.

## TC-10 — Color mismatch visual test
**Expected result:** Compare pink-heavy creative with approved navy/teal; treat as review signal.

> Beautiful Brand Stories — Turn ideas into impact with AI-powered content governance.
